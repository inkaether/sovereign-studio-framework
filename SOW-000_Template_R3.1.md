### 2.1 Synthesis Architecture

The Consultant operates a cross-domain synthesis framework. Decision Artifacts are produced through a governed folding process:

| Layer | Direction | Function | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wellness Layer** | Top-Down | Purpose, capacity, nervous system alignment | Wellness |
| **Strategy Layer** | Top-Down | Positioning, business direction, market entry | Strategy |
| **Design Layer** | Top-Down ↔︎ Bottom-Up | Visual identity, UX/UI, spatial articulation | Design |
| **The Fold** | Synthesis | Where human interpretation meets AI generation | Cross-domain |
| **Data Layer** | Bottom-Up | Pattern recognition, context absorption | All |
| **Synthesis Materials** | Bottom-Up | AI-generated raw elements (pre-curation) | All |

> **Boundary:** The Stakeholder licenses outputs of **The Fold** (curated, ratified Decision Artifacts). **Synthesis Materials** (pre-fold inputs) remain Consultant property.
> 

### 2.2 Value Distribution (Human-AI Governance)

| What AI Does (Bottom-Up) | What Consultant Does (Top-Down) |
| --- | --- |
| Generates raw visual/textual elements | Selects WHICH elements carry correct cultural weight |
| Pattern-matches training data | Assigns strategic significance |
| Produces 40-60% of material volume | Assembles, curates, embeds narrative (40-60% of value) |
| Outputs aesthetic-neutral options | Injects political/aesthetic stance |
| Creates disconnected parts | Composes connected story |

> **Value Proposition:** The value lies in the **interpretation**, not the generation. Anyone can prompt an AI. Not everyone possesses the judgment to know which output carries the correct cultural weight for this specific Stakeholder context.

## SECTION 3: VALIDATION CYCLE PROTOCOL (ITERATION DIAMOND)

> *Per MSA §2.2: An Iteration Cycle represents one complete iteration of Submit → Review → Feedback → Adjustment. Exactly **two (2) Iteration Cycles** are permitted per major milestone.*
> 

### 3.1 Decision Gate Definition

| Term | Perspective | Function | Contractual Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Validation Milestone** | Client-facing | “Approve or reject” | 2 cycles max |
| **Decision Gate** | Operational/Internal | “Lock direction + release funds” | Payment + next phase unlock |

**The Connection:**
1. **Validation Milestone** (Stakeholder confirms “this is the right direction”)
↓
2. **Decision Gate Opens** (Contract triggers: payment due, phase lock, next phase authorized)
↓
3. **If Rejected** → Second Validation Milestone (last cycle)
↓
4. **If Rejected Again** → Decision Gate Closes → **Change Order Required**

**Why Two Names?**
| Reason | Validation Milestone | Decision Gate |
| :— | :— | :— |
| **Language** | Collaborative (invites participation) | Contractual (defines obligations) |
| **Focus** | Quality/Truth (Are we interpreting correctly?) | Commercial/Operational (Money, timeline, scope) |
| **Risk** | Shared responsibility for feedback | Protection against infinite loops |

**A Decision Gate locks when ONE of the following is met:**
| Condition | Mechanism | Effect |
| :— | :— | :— |
| **Explicit Acceptance** | Written confirmation of alignment with Strategic Thesis | Artifact ratified. Payment triggers. |
| **Deemed Approval** | Failure to provide specific, actionable feedback within `[X]` business days | Artifact auto-ratified. Payment triggers. |
| **Cycle Exhaustion** | Rejection after Cycle 2 without triggering a Change Order | Artifact stands as submitted. Payment triggers. |

> **Once a Decision Gate locks, the ratified direction cannot be altered without a Change Order for Strategic Re-Alignment (Section 5).**
> 

### 3.2 Iteration Diamond (Double-Diamond Alignment)

Each milestone traverses the Iteration Diamond before reaching its Decision Gate:

Layer 1: DISCOVER + DEFINE Layer 2: DEVELOP Layer 3: DELIVER (divergent → convergent) (convergent → divergent) (divergent → curated convergence) │ │ │ ▼ ▼ ▼ Context Absorption Translation + Drafting Curated Synthesis (patterns, signals) (framework, blueprint) (final Decision Artifact) │ │ │ └────────── Cycle 1 ─────────────────┘ │ ↕ Validation │ └────────── Cycle 2 ───────────────────────────────────────────────┘ ↕ Validation │ ▼ ═══ DECISION GATE ═══ │ ▼ LOCKED ✓ (payment + next phase)

| Milestone 1 | Strategic Diagnosis (PDF/Notion) | `[Date/Trigger]` | `$[AMOUNT]` upon Validation |

### 3.4 Validation Rules

- Each Milestone permits exactly **two (2) Iteration Cycles**.
- Feedback must be **consolidated** (not drip-fed). Individual comments sent across multiple channels will not reset the Validation Clock.
- Feedback must address the **Strategic Thesis**, not surface aesthetics. “The colors feel off” is insufficient. “This direction does not reflect our stated market position” is actionable.
- Failure to validate within **`[X]`** business days = Automatic Acceptance (Decision Gate Locks per Section 3.1).

### 3.5 Post-Validation Consequence

Once a Decision Gate locks, any subsequent request to alter the ratified direction constitutes a **Strategic Re-Alignment** requiring a Change Order (Section 5). This protects the integrity of the work already commissioned and compensates the Consultant for the reinterpretation labor required—including mandatory Context Absorption re-engagement.
