## SECTION 1.0: GOVERNANCE & SCOPE OF RELATIONSHIP

3. **Curated Synthesis (Phase 3):** Assembly, narrative embedding, political/aesthetic framing

The **Visual Architecture Guide (Annexes A, B, C)** is incorporated by reference into this MSA and applies to all SOWs. Exclusion of Annex D does not alter the binding effect of Decision Gates (Annex A §2) or payment obligations (MSA §3.1); it merely excludes dimensional payment tracking. All other definitions remain in full force regardless of Annex D inclusion.

### 1.3 Hierarchy Rule (MSA/SOW Conflict Resolution)

In the event of any direct conflict between the terms of this MSA and any SOW, **the terms of the MSA shall control**. This ensures that project-specific language cannot override core corporate liability protections, data sovereignty policies, or IP ownership structures.

### 1.4 Mutual Non-Extraction Alignment

Consistent with the Studio's Mutual Non-Extraction Agreement:

- Initial exploratory dialogue (prior to MSA signing) is considered open information; neither party claims exclusive ownership over abstract ideas sparked during resonance-seeking conversations.
- Upon MSA execution, Protected Implementation frameworks (double-diamond methodology, proprietary pipelines, execution mechanics) become binding.
- **Cognitive Sovereignty (Memory Conservation Protocol):** Concepts, techniques, or insights naturally retained in the unaided memory of either party remain part of that individual's sovereign cognitive ecosystem. No agreement shall penalize natural learning or restrict future independent creative evolution.

### 1.6 Terminology Definitions

For purposes of this Agreement and all SOWs issued hereunder, the following definitions apply:

**1. Decision Artifact**  
A ratified strategic output representing Consultant's judgment, formally accepted by Stakeholder through Decision Gate protocol. Distinct from raw deliverables; embodies truth-testing and contextual framing. Each Decision Artifact coincides with a defined Milestone in the governing SOW. Upon full payment, Decision Artifacts transfer to Stakeholder; all working files remain Consultant property per Section 2.3.

**2. Decision Gate**  
The formal checkpoint where a Decision Artifact is either Accepted or Deemed Approved. A Decision Gate locks upon: (a) Stakeholder written acceptance after Iteration Cycle 2; OR (b) Passage of **[X]** business days post-submission without written rejection with specific actionable feedback (per SOW Section 3). Once locked, the corresponding Milestone payment becomes due per Section 3.1.

**3. Iteration Cycle ("Iteration Diamond")**  
One complete iteration of Submit → Review → Feedback → Adjustment per major milestone, aligned with the Double Diamond framework (Divergent Exploration → Convergent Judgment → Divergent Synthesis → Convergent Ratification). Implies truth-checking of interpretation, not aesthetic refinement. Exactly two (2) Iteration Cycles are permitted per milestone. Exhaustion triggers Decision Gate lock or Strategic Re-Alignment.

**4. Strategic Re-Alignment**  
Any fundamental shift in the Core Interpretation Thesis ("the Why") after Iteration Cycle 2. Requires return to Context Absorption mode to reassess forward progress. Treated as new engagement; requires Change Order under Section 5, assessed at full stakes × depth rate for re-interpretation labor.

**5. Synthesis Materials**  
AI-generated intermediate inputs requiring human curation and compositional judgment. Includes raw outputs, prompt logs, discarded drafts, unselected variations, and AI synthesis layers. Remain exclusive property of Consultant (see Section 2.3). Distinct from Decision Artifacts, which represent ratified final outputs.

**6. Stakeholder**  
Party engaging Consultant's services; implies shared risk/responsibility in outcome (preferred over "Client"). Enters MSA/SOW relationship with expectation of collaborative truth-testing, not transactional delivery.

**7. Consultant**  
Party providing Sovereign Interpretation Services; retains ownership of methodologies, Synthesis Materials, judgment logic, and double-diamond execution frameworks.

**8. Context Absorption**  
Phase 1 of the Triple-Layer Interpretation Model. Involves raw ingestion, pattern clustering, and anomaly flagging before Pattern Recognition (Phase 2) and Curated Synthesis (Phase 3). Required for initial engagement and Strategic Re-Alignment scenarios.

**9. Double Diamond Framework**  
The execution methodology governing all engagements (published openly under CC-BY-SA 4.0 as part of the Sovereign Studio Framework):
- Layer 1 (Discover + Define): Divergent → Convergent (high judgment, positioning diagnosis)
- Layer 2 (Develop): Convergent → Divergent (translation, framework development)
- Layer 3 (Deliver): Divergent exploration → Convergent synthesis (curated convergence)
Each Iteration Cycle operates within one of these layers; Strategic Re-Alignment may require return to Layer 1.

### 1.7 Feasibility Studies & Research Gates

**1.7.1 Definition of Feasibility Phase:** Certain engagements may commence with a distinct **Feasibility Phase** (hereinafter "Phase 1"), dedicated solely to validating the strategic, financial, and operational viability of a proposed initiative. Phase 1 is distinct from full-scale execution (Phase 2) and is governed by the following protocols:

**1.7.2 Deliverable: The Decision Artifact:** Upon completion of Phase 1, the Consultant shall deliver a **Feasibility Dossier** (the "Decision Artifact"). This Dossier shall include, but is not limited to:
- (a) Strategic Viability: Alignment with Stakeholder goals and market positioning.
- (b) Financial Modeling: ROI projections, breakeven analysis, and cost-benefit assessment.
- (c) Operational & Wellness Audit: Risk assessment, capacity analysis, safety protocols, and nervous system/cultural friction impact.
- (d) Recommendation: A binary Go/No-Go recommendation based on pre-defined viability thresholds.

**1.7.3 The Gate Review Protocol:**
- **Trigger:** Phase 1 concludes upon delivery of the Feasibility Dossier.
- **Acceptance:** Stakeholder acceptance of the Dossier (or passage of **[X]** business days without rejection per Section 2.2) triggers the **Decision Gate**.
- **Funding Condition:** Acceptance of the Dossier is a **condition precedent** to the release of funds for Phase 2 (Execution).
- **Cancellation Right:** If the Feasibility Dossier determines that the initiative fails to meet the financial viability thresholds or risk tolerance levels defined in the SOW, the Stakeholder retains the unconditional right to **terminate Phase 2 without penalty**. No further fees shall be due for Phase 2, and the Feasibility Phase fee remains non-refundable as compensation for the due diligence performed.

**1.7.4 Intellectual Property & Data Sovereignty:**
- **Ownership of Output:** Upon full payment of Phase 1 fees, the Stakeholder receives exclusive rights to the **Feasibility Dossier** and the specific data sets generated therein.
- **Retention of Methodology:** The Consultant retains all rights to the **Feasibility Framework**, including the templates, algorithms, scoring matrices, and synthesis logic used to generate the Dossier. These remain the exclusive property of the Consultant and are licensed to the Stakeholder only for the purpose of reviewing the specific Dossier.
- **Synthesis Materials:** All raw research notes, intermediate drafts, prompt logs, and unselected synthesis materials generated during Phase 1 remain the exclusive property of the Consultant.

**1.7.5 Classification as R&D Capex:** The Parties acknowledge that Phase 1 constitutes **Research & Development (R&D)** activity. The fees associated with this phase are classified as **Capital Expenditures (Capex)** for the Stakeholder, intended to mitigate risk and validate the investment thesis prior to committing to Phase 2 operational expenditures (Opex).

## SECTION 2.0: OPERATIONAL PROTOCOLS & GUARDRAILS

### 2.1 Data Hygiene & Security Protocol

Stakeholder agrees to maintain a separation of duties regarding data storage. Operational collaboration tools (e.g., Notion, Slack, Email) shall not contain sensitive PII, financial records, private keys, or executable source code. All sensitive assets must be transmitted via End-to-End Encrypted (E2EE) channels designated by Consultant (e.g., Proton Drive) and linked as references within operational tools. Consultant reserves the right to suspend work if sensitive data is deposited in non-compliant channels.

### 2.2 Iteration Cycles (i.e., Revisions)

> **Cycle Definition:** An **Iteration Cycle** (or "Iteration Diamond") represents one complete iteration of Submit → Review → Feedback → Adjustment per major milestone. Exactly **two (2) Iteration Cycles are permitted per major milestone within each phase**. This reflects the inherent truth-testing required to arrive at accurate interpretation—not endless pixel refinement.
> 1. Consultant submits Decision Artifact
> 2. Stakeholder reviews against Strategic Thesis (the "Why")
> 3. Stakeholder provides consolidated feedback
> 4. Consultant adjusts and resubmits

**Post-Cycle Protocol:**
- **Acceptance:** If Stakeholder confirms alignment with the Strategic Thesis, the Decision Gate locks. Payment triggers. Next phase unlocks.
- **Rejection:** If Stakeholder rejects after Iteration Cycle 2, this constitutes a **Strategic Re-Alignment**. The Core Interpretation Thesis has shifted fundamentally. A formal Change Order is required (see Section 5), assessed at the full stakes × depth rate for re-interpretation.

**Deemed Approval:**
If Stakeholder fails to respond with specific, actionable feedback within **[X]** business days of submission, the Decision Artifact is deemed Accepted. The Decision Gate locks automatically. Payment becomes due. Next phase proceeds without further delay.

**Rationale:**
This structure protects both parties—the Stakeholder gets meaningful opportunity to test interpretive truth; the Consultant maintains operational boundaries against infinite iteration loops that commoditize judgment.

### 2.3 Intellectual Property & AI Disclosure

**AI Utilization Statement:** The Consultant employs artificial intelligence models as computational substrates for generating raw synthesis materials. These materials function as intermediate inputs—not final outputs.

**Value Distribution:**
- **Generation (AI Layer):** AI produces raw visual/textual elements through pattern matching on training data.
- **Curation (Human Layer):** The Consultant applies Sovereign Judgment—cultural, political, aesthetic, and strategic framing—to select, arrange, and imbue meaning into these materials.

**What You Are Licensing:** The Stakeholder acquires rights to the **Final Decision Artifacts**—the curated, ratified outputs representing the Consultant's judgment. This license does not extend to:
- Raw synthesis materials (unselected AI outputs)
- Prompt logs or model weights
- Intermediate drafts discarded during curation
- The underlying selection logic (the "why")

*Upon full payment,* Stakeholder receives exclusive rights to Final Decision Artifacts as outlined in the SOW. **Working files, intermediate drafts, source code, prompt logs, and AI-generated raw assets ("Synthesis Materials") remain Consultant property.**

### 2.4 Data Residency & File Architecture

**2.4.1 Sensitive Asset Classification:** All documents containing the following shall be stored exclusively in Proton Drive:
- Executed contracts, NDAs, and amendments
- Financial data (banking, invoicing, wire instructions, crypto wallet addresses)
- Personal Identifiable Information (PII): SSN, EIN, Tax IDs
- Stakeholder proprietary source code or trade secrets

**2.4.2 Folder Structure Compliance:** The Consultant maintains the following vault architecture:
- [00] STUDIO_INFRASTRUCTURE/ — Permanent governance templates
- [01] STAKEHOLDERS/ — Per-Stakeholder workspaces (per SOW project numbering)
- [99] ARCHIVE/ — Post-termination retention (7-year minimum)

**2.4.3 Cross-Platform Data Separation:** Operational tools (Notion, Airtable, Slack) may contain project metadata, milestones, trackers, and communication logs. However, NONE of these platforms shall host sensitive assets listed in 2.4.1. References only (Proton Drive links) may be embedded in operational tools.

**2.4.4 AI Processing Boundaries:** External AI platforms (Midjourney, Gemini, Lumo) receive only de-identified, non-sensitive material. Stakeholder names, PII, financial data, and proprietary source code must be redacted or abstracted before any AI ingestion.

### 2.5 Post-Delivery Asset Access

**2.5.1 Delivery Window:** All Final Decision Artifacts will be delivered via Consultant's Secure Vault (Proton Drive E2EE). Stakeholder will receive a **Time-Limited Access Link** with download access for **30 days post-final payment clearance**.

**2.5.2 Archive Storage:** After the 30-day window, Stakeholder is responsible for maintaining their own local copies. Consultant archives all project files for a **7-year minimum retention period** per industry standards, accessible upon request for a nominal retrieval fee of $500.

**2.5.3 Source Files:** Original project files, AI synthesis layers, prompt logs, and intermediate drafts remain archived in Consultant's Secure Vault. These are not transferable unless separately negotiated as a paid license (per MSA §2.3).

#### (d) BREACH OF FLOW PROTOCOL

**(i) Definition.** A "Breach of Flow" occurs when Stakeholder fails to clear any outstanding invoice within twenty-four (24) calendar hours following an Event-Based Settlement milestone, including but not limited to Decision Gate lock, scheduled retainer auto-processing, or smart contract escrow release trigger.

**(ii) Tiered Enforcement.** Upon Breach of Flow, Consultant may execute graduated operational suspension:

| Time Elapsed | Status | Access Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0–24 Hours** | Active + Alert | Full collaborative access maintained |
| **24–72 Hours** | Warning Phase | Automated notification sent. No restrictions yet |
| **72–96 Hours** | Read-Only Mode | Shared workspaces (Slack, Notion, Proton Drive folders) set to read-only. No new collaboration permitted |
| **96+ Hours** | Full Suspension | All calendars blocked, advisory sessions cancelled, active strategy syncs halted. Work paused until account cleared |

**(iii) Liability Waiver.** Stakeholder acknowledges that any project timeline guarantees established in an active SOW are automatically voided and subject to renegotiation upon Breach of Flow-triggered suspension. Consultant bears zero liability for delays, milestone overruns, or data losses resulting directly or indirectly from Breach of Flow suspension.

**(iv) Re-Mobilization.** Prior to reactivation of services, access, or scheduling following Breach of Flow, Stakeholder must (a) completely cure the underlying financial delinquency, and (b) pay the Re-Absorption/Re-Mobilization Fee specified in Section 3.1(a) or (b), whichever is applicable.

#### (e) Late Fees

Overdue balances beyond any applicable grace period are subject to a late fee of **1.5% per month** (18% APR) on the outstanding principal amount. Interest compounds daily from the original due date until paid in full. This accrual is in addition to Breach of Flow suspension rights described above.

#### (f) Transaction Fees

Stakeholder shall bear all transaction costs associated with payment transmission, including but not limited to international wire transfer routing fees, credit card processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction), cryptocurrency network gas fees, and any intermediary bank charges. The final cleared amount deposited into Consultant's account must equal the exact face value of the invoice. Any shortfall will be invoiced separately and must be remitted within seven (7) days.

#### (g) Cryptocurrency Volatility

For payments made via digital assets (Bitcoin, Ethereum, or other cryptocurrencies), the USD valuation rate is locked at the exact timestamp of invoice issuance. Stakeholder assumes all risk of asset price fluctuation between invoice issuance and payment clearance. For smart contract escrow arrangements, the contract must specify the stablecoin denomination (e.g., USDC, USDT) to eliminate volatility risk during the funding period.

#### (h) Refund Policy

All upfront deposits, Context Absorption Fees, and milestone payments made for completed phases are **strictly non-refundable**. This policy protects the reservation of Consultant's operational bandwidth, contextual absorption labor, and opportunity cost of turned-away work. The sole exception: if Consultant materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within thirty (30) days of written notice, Stakeholder may seek proportional refund of unearned fees only.
